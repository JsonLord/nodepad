# Developing brain export

Exports separate `knowledge/`, active `hypotheses/`, and negative/historical `archive/`. Promotion requires a qualified band, confidence ≥70, two source types, freshness and no contest. Falling below a gate demotes prior knowledge and appends an audit event. Disqualified hypotheses remain negative knowledge.

Every export has a manifest, summary, graph and changelog. Gardener output is proposal-only and cannot bypass promotion gates.
