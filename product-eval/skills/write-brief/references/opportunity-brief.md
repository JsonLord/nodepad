# Opportunity Brief, structure & question bank

Two renderings of the same content. Neutral naming; "narrative style" is the press-release-from-the-future approach without the label.

## Narrative / report version

Order:
1. Headline + one-line subhead (what changes, for whom).
2. Opening paragraph, what's proposed/launching + quantified value + the previous tradeoff it removes.
3. How it works, in one realistic workflow.
4. Before → after contrast.
5. Customer voice (before → now → outcome).
6. Business impact (revenue / cost / strategic), with the top assumption named.
7. Success metrics (north star + guardrail): baseline → target → window.
8. The ask, decision requested, owner, date.

Rules: prose, no bullets in the final output; one quantified claim in the first two paragraphs; explicit before/after; no unsupported roadmap commitments; active voice with named actors; future tense only for approved commitments.

Starters:
- "Today {persona} must {workaround}, costing {quantified impact}."
- "{Product} now lets {persona} {job} without {tradeoff}, improving {metric} from {baseline} to {target}."
- "Before X we {workaround}; now we {new workflow}, which {outcome}.", {Name, Title, Company}

## FAQ version (the completeness gate)

Answer every relevant question; "Not provided in input" when a value is missing. External questions then internal, continuous numbering.

Core questions:
1. What is this and who is it for?
2. What customer problem does it solve, and what evidence supports it?
3. Why now? (the trigger)
4. How does it work at a high level?
5. How is it different from the alternatives, including the status quo / workaround?
6. Which use cases should NOT use it?
7. What's explicitly out of scope, and why?
8. What are the key limitations / known gaps?
9. What are the primary failure modes and mitigations?
10. How will we measure success (north star + guardrails), with baseline and target?
11. What are the top risks, owners, and mitigations?
12. Which decisions are one-way doors?
13. What does it cost / what's the rough economics?
14. What's the migration path from today's workflow?

Add context-pack questions (platform, security/privacy, GTM, economics) by relevance. Answer rules: customer-facing answers concise (2-4 sentences); don't invent quantitative values; every claim traceable to evidence in the appendix.

## Appendix

Evidence summary (source · strength · recency), economics assumptions, cohort/journey notes. Appendices carry evidence, not marketing.


## Decision-memo version (`--style memo`)

For driving a decision by a deadline (not persuasion). Headings in order:
1. Purpose & decision requested, one explicit sentence + a deadline.
2. Executive summary, customer + business impact.
3. Current state & evidence, each claim with source + strength.
4. Root cause, the cause, not a restated symptom.
5. Options considered, ≥3 including do-nothing: `Option | Summary | Benefits | Tradeoffs | Key risk | Why not selected`.
6. Recommended strategy, falsifiable success/failure criteria.
7. Metrics scorecard, `Metric | Baseline | Target | Owner | Date`.
8. Economics (if relevant), gross → net → margin; sensitivities.
9. Risks, dependencies, mitigations, owner + close date.
10. The ask, concrete, owner- and time-bound.
