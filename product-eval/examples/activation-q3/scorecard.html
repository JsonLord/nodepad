How to read this
Value (0 to 100)
If this problem is real, how big is it? Built from persona weight, funnel location, frequency, and competitive pressure (each 0 to 25). It is about potential impact, not certainty.
Confidence (0 to 100)
How sure are we the problem is real and worth acting on? Computed from total evidence weight, source-type diversity, and unresolved contradictions. Reported as a band (Low / Moderate / High); the number is for audit.
Evidence weight
Quality-weighted volume of evidence, deduplicated by account. Three strong first-party findings outweigh fifty vague mentions, so weight, not raw count, drives Confidence.
Accounts affected
Distinct accounts after identity resolution (one customer across Zendesk, CRM, and analytics counts once), so a single loud account is not mistaken for a widespread problem.
The recommendation tiers
Decide now: worth building and well supported. Run a research sprint: high value but needs more proof, design the cheapest validating test. Do not commit yet: too unproven to act on. Deprioritize: prize too small to pursue.
